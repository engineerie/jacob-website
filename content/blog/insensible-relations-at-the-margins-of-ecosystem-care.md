In May 2023, I spent a month at Mustarinda in Finland with artist and ecological surveyor George Ridgway, exploring the challenges of sensing and representing ecological life. Drawing on our time in the surrounding old-growth forest, this conversation considers what biodiversity data and digital models make visible, what remains beyond their reach, and how we might care for species and relationships that resist detection or representation.
