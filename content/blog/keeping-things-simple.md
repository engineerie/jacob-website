*This is a sample post.*

Making something simple is often harder than adding another thing.

A useful starting question is **what does this need to do?** It is easy to lose that question once the options start multiplying.

For a personal website, the answer can be modest:

- Introduce the person behind it.
- Make it easy to find their writing.
- Give the words enough room to breathe.

Everything else can wait until there is a reason for it.
