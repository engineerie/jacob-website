*This is a sample post.*

There is something useful about having a place to put an idea before it feels finished. A notebook does that privately. A website can do it in the open.

## Start with something small

A short observation. A question that keeps coming back. Something learned while making something else. Not every piece of writing needs a grand conclusion.

The point is to pay attention, then leave a little record of it.
